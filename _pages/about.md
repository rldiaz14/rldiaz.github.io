Hi! I’m Ramon Diaz, a Master’s student in Computer Science at Drexel University focusing on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. My work centers around building intelligent systems that interact with virtual environments, especially in areas like reinforcement learning, simulation, and game development. I appreciate combining creativity and technical problem-solving to develop systems that can learn, adapt, and support interactive experiences. \\

Currently, I am working on projects involving game AI in Unreal Engine, reinforcement learning agents, and web-based academic tools. I aim to keep exploring the boundaries of intelligent behavior in research and creative applications.\\

Outside of school and work, I enjoy exploring the future of immersive storytelling, learning chess strategies, and building automation tools that empower others. I aim to pursue a Ph.D. in AI for games and simulations while exploring real-world applications like smart infrastructure and interactive AI experiences.
